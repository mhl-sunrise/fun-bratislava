iPad Treasure Hunt

A treasure hunt through the Old Town

Teams with a tablet, tasks on a map and the city as the board. You will walk past places you have passed for years without noticing. It ends with the results — and usually a surprise about which team won.

Duration90 – 120 minutes Group4 – 30 people Price€33 / person

What is included

  • Tablets for every team
  • Route and tasks prepared in advance
  • A guide at the start and the finish
  • Results and a certificate for the winners

What you can add

  • Guiding in English or German by agreement
  • Photographer 60 €

Start and finish in the Old Town, all within walking distance. It runs in any weather — if it rains we adjust the route.
